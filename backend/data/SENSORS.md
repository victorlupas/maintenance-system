## Air Compressor
Source: Kaggle – Predictive Maintenance Dataset (Air Compressor)

Sensors used:
- rpm
- motor_power
- torque
- outlet_pressure_bar
- air_flow
- outlet_temp
- noise_db
- vibration (gaccz)

Condition indicators:
- bearings
- radiator
- exvalve
- acmotor

## Milling Machine (AI4I 2020)
Source: Kaggle – AI4I 2020 Predictive Maintenance Dataset

Sensors used:
- air_temperature
- process_temperature
- rotational_speed
- torque
- tool_wear

Failure indicators:
- machine_failure
- twf, hdf, pwf, osf, rnf

## Turbofan Engine (NASA C-MAPSS FD001)
Source: NASA C-MAPSS

Sensors used:
- s2  (fan inlet temperature)
- s3  (fan inlet pressure)
- s4  (LPC outlet temperature)
- s7  (fan speed)
- s11 (fuel flow)
- s15 (bypass pressure)
- s21 (vibration proxy)

Characteristic:
- Progressive degradation over time
- No explicit failure label
- Failure occurs at end of sequence
